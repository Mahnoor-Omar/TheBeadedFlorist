Our story

The story behind The Beaded Florist.

From a wilted bouquet to beaded flowers

The Beaded Florist began in Lahore after a friend’s fresh bouquet wilted. Shiza started making flowers from glass beads that would never fade. She learned from Pinterest, YouTube tutorials and a lot of trial and error. Her first sale was at a bakery launch.
